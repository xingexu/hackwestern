Lock rotation first

Hold your phone upright and lock the screen in portrait.

  • iPhoneOpen Control Centre and turn on Portrait Orientation Lock.
  • AndroidOpen Quick Settings and turn off Auto-rotate.

Tap Start driving and allow motion access. Hold the phone sideways like a wheel: turn clockwise for right, counterclockwise for left. Double-tap Brake in a turn to drift.

For a steady layout, hold the phone upright, lock rotation, then turn it sideways again.
